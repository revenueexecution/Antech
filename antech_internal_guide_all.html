1. Solution Overview

ReSolve is a real-time HPLC mobile phase recycler. It monitors the detector signal during an isocratic run, diverts true chromatographic peaks to waste, and returns uncontaminated mobile phase between peaks directly to the solvent reservoir for reuse — without altering the validated method, hardware, or pump.

>90%mobile phase recycled
€12,300typical annual savings/unit
3–6 motypical payback

2. Market

Any regulated lab running isocratic HPLC methods: pharmaceutical QC/QA, biopharma, CDMOs, and — secondary but real — food/feed testing and chemical manufacturing labs running routine HPLC. Three converging market drivers make this a live conversation right now:

  • Rising solvent purchase and hazardous waste disposal costs
  • Corporate ESG/sustainability reporting mandates creating internal pressure to show measurable waste reduction
  • Increasing regulatory scrutiny on data integrity (ALCOA+) — ReSolve's automated, timestamped audit trail is a stronger answer than manual solvent-saving workarounds some labs already improvise
